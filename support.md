# Tomas Support

For synchronization help:

1. Confirm that the Mac and iPhone use the same iCloud account.
2. Open Tomas on both devices and keep the Mac app in the foreground while
   testing synchronization.
3. On iPhone, open the information menu and choose **Sync details** to review
   the account, store, and CloudKit activity.
4. Remember that automations, agents, and MCP run on the Mac. Their task changes
   can synchronize to iPhone, but the iPhone does not execute agents.

If the issue continues, open a
[support request](https://github.com/aredesrafa/tomas-support/issues/new). Include
the Tomas version, device model, OS version, and the diagnostic status. Do not
post task content, attachment contents, credentials, tokens, or your Apple ID.
