# Tomas Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026.

Tomas stores the tasks, notes, Tags, checklists, attachments, and related
content you create on your devices and in the private CloudKit database of your
Apple iCloud account. This synchronization is an app feature operated by Apple.
The Tomas developer does not receive or have access to the content in your
private CloudKit database.

Tomas does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
The developer does not collect personal data or user-created content through
the app, does not sell it, and does not share it with third parties.

Technical diagnostics remain on your device. They may include task UUIDs,
timestamps, synchronization operation types and statuses, and Apple framework
error domains and codes. They do not include task titles, notes, Tags,
checklists, attachment contents, account fingerprints, authentication tokens,
or credentials. Tomas sends no diagnostic telemetry to the developer.

You control retention through Tomas and iCloud. Deleting tasks or attachments
in Tomas synchronizes those changes through your private iCloud database. You
can also manage or delete Tomas data using Apple's iCloud settings. Because the
developer cannot access your private database, the developer cannot retrieve or
delete that data on your behalf.

If you voluntarily share a screenshot, diagnostic excerpt, or other content
when requesting support, it is used only to respond to that request. Do not post
private task content, credentials, or tokens in a public issue.

Tomas is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's
personal information. This policy may be updated when Tomas's data practices
change; the current revision date will appear above.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the
[Tomas support tracker](https://github.com/aredesrafa/tomas-support/issues).
